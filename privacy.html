Privacy policy

Project-X Drive Sync. Last updated 26 November 2026.

What this tool is

Project-X Drive Sync is a private tool that keeps one shared Google Drive folder and a folder on its owner's computer in step, using the open-source program rclone. It is used by its owner and a few invited collaborators.

What it accesses

With your permission, the tool accesses files and folders in your Google Drive. It uses that access only to list, download, upload, update and remove files in the shared project folder, so that the folder on the computer and the folder in Google Drive match.

Where the data goes

Files are copied directly between Google Drive and the computer running the tool. There is no server in between. The sign-in token issued by Google is stored on that computer only.

What it does not do

  • It does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
  • It does not use your data for advertising.
  • It does not use your data to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person reads your files through this tool, other than the people the Drive folder is already shared with.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access stops the sync. Copies of files already on the computer stay there until its owner deletes them.
